Request access to the AXIOS EQUITY portals.

Access to our Investor and Partner Portals is invitation-only and reserved for qualified institutional investors, accredited investors, operating partners, and authorised counterparties.

Eligibility

Who can request access?

Each portal serves a distinct constituency. Please confirm that you fall within one of the categories below before submitting a request.

Investor Portal

For qualified investors

Investor Portal access is reserved for individuals and entities that qualify under one or more of the following:

  • Qualified institutional buyer (QIB)
  • Qualified purchaser
  • Accredited investor under U.S. securities laws
  • Professional investor under applicable non-U.S. law
  • Authorised representative of a family office
Partner Portal

For operating partners

Partner Portal access is reserved for parties engaged in an active workstream with AXIOS EQUITY:

  • Authorised representative of a portfolio company
  • Operating partner under a written agreement
  • Counterparty to an active transaction
  • Advisor engaged by AXIOS EQUITY
Onboarding

What to expect

After you submit the form below, our team will:

  1. Verify your eligibility and existing engagement
  2. Conduct any necessary KYC / AML checks
  3. Issue your portal credentials by email within 2–3 business days
  4. Schedule an introductory call if required

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

How long does the access review take?

Most requests are reviewed within 2–3 business days. Complex cases involving KYC/AML verification or non-U.S. jurisdictions may take 5–7 business days. You will receive an acknowledgment of your submission and an outcome notification by email.

Will my email address be confirmed before access is granted?

Yes. Once approved, your portal credentials will be linked to the exact email address you submit. You will receive a confirmation email and will need to use that same address to log in. Login is verified by a one-time PIN sent to that address each session.

What if I am referred by an existing investor or partner?

Please include the name of your referrer in the "AXIOS EQUITY relationship partner" field on the form. We will reach out to the referrer to confirm before granting access.

What information will I see in the portal once granted access?

Each user is granted scoped access to specific projects (Investor Portal) or workstreams (Partner Portal) relevant to their engagement. You will only see materials directly related to your authorisation — not the full universe of AXIOS EQUITY activity.

What if I am denied access?

If you do not meet eligibility criteria or your relationship with AXIOS EQUITY is not yet established, we may decline the request and suggest alternative engagement paths. You may reapply at a later date if your circumstances change.

Is my information stored securely?

Submission data is used solely for access review and is handled under our privacy notice. We do not share submission data with third parties, and we use enterprise-grade authentication (Cloudflare Access) to protect the portal itself.
