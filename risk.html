Risk & Liquidity · Operating Framework

Risk run from
one book.

The platform's risk management is integrated — a single CRO function aggregates exposures across every asset class, every counterparty, and every position. Discipline at the platform level is what makes specialization at the asset level safe.

Risk Principles

Four operating commitments.

i.

Single risk book

Exposures are aggregated across asset classes — not measured in silos. Correlations, factor tilts, and concentration are tracked at the integrated level.

ii.

Liquidity over yield

Treasury maintains a deliberate liquidity ladder. We accept lower yield to keep deployable capital available when opportunities open or stress emerges.

iii.

Counterparty discipline

All counterparties — exchanges, brokers, custodians, OTC desks — are reviewed quarterly. Concentration limits apply at counterparty level, not just position level.

iv.

Stress before sizing

Every position is sized against multiple historical stress scenarios before deployment — not against normal-times volatility alone.

Position & Concentration Limits

Hard limits at the platform level.

The CRO sets platform-level limits that apply across all asset classes. Individual asset class leads operate within their portion of the limits, and may not breach without CIO override.

Liquidity Laddering

Treasury keeps capital deployable.

Treasury operates an explicit liquidity ladder — cash, near-cash, short duration liquid public market positions, and longer-dated reserves. The ladder is sized against forward commitments and against the platform's expected stress redemption profile.

Stress Testing

Five scenarios run continuously.

i.

2008 GFC analog

Liquidity collapse, credit spreads blowout, equity drawdown, commodity demand shock. Tests correlation breakdown.

ii.

1970s stagflation

Persistent inflation, monetary policy whiplash, commodity boom, real-rate volatility. Tests real-asset exposures.

iii.

2020 pandemic

Synchronized global growth shock, oil price collapse, fiscal response, asset reflation. Tests demand shock and policy speed.

iv.

EM crisis

USD strength, EM FX collapse, sovereign distress, capital outflows. Tests jurisdictional concentration.

v.

Crypto winter

Digital asset 70-80% drawdown, infrastructure stress, counterparty failures. Tests digital book sizing.
