Sector Commentary

What we are watching, by market.

Energy

Tightening physical balances

OPEC+ production discipline, slower US shale capex growth, and structural demand from emerging markets sustain a constructive crude backdrop. Natural gas remains a story of seasonal demand and storage geometry. We hold exposure across crude, gas, and refined products.

Real assets

Cap rate stabilization, sponsor stress

Commercial real estate has substantially repriced. We are selective — preferred equity and structured credit on cash-flowing assets with experienced sponsors. Logistics and Class A residential remain the most defensible exposures.

Mining

Critical minerals as a multi-year theme

Energy transition demand for copper, lithium, and nickel continues to outrun supply response. Selective in lithium pricing risk; structural conviction on copper and uranium. Royalty and streaming structures dominate position sizing.

Equities

Concentration and event-driven

Index-level valuation leaves little margin. We hold concentrated long positions and selectively pursue event-driven situations — spin-offs, restructurings, capital-return inflections — where business quality justifies sizing through the cycle.

Rates & Credit

Quality over yield

Spread compression in credit leaves limited downside protection. We hold duration in sovereigns and high-grade corporates, with underweight stance on high-yield. Private credit selectively, with strong covenants and recovery rights.

Digital

Cycle leadership, institutional flows

Bitcoin's macro role as a digital reserve continues to develop. Ethereum's institutional adoption and stablecoin economics provide a more anchored thesis. Selective infrastructure positions; venture-stage exposure limited and structured carefully.

Macro Anchor

The framework beneath the view.

All sector views sit within an integrated macro framework — growth, inflation, real rates, and policy. The framework anchors position sizing across the platform.

Full macro outlook →
Growth

Decelerating but resilient

US growth slower than 2022-23 cycle peak but supported by services consumption and AI capex. Europe stable, China policy-led recovery uncertain.

Inflation

Stalling above target

Goods deflation complete; services inflation sticky. Labor markets cooling. Path to 2% remains uneven; risk of policy patience extending into 2027.

Policy

Asynchronous easing

ECB and BoC ahead of Fed in cutting cycle. BoJ exiting accommodation. EM CBs constrained by FX. USD anchored but not strong.
