Banks, insurers, market infrastructure.

We cover the regulated financial system in depth — banks, insurers, asset managers, market infrastructure, and the next generation of regulated digital platforms.

"Regulated capital is harder to allocate well — and more rewarding when one does."

Sector coverage.

Our financial services coverage runs across the full breadth of regulated financial businesses: commercial and investment banks, insurance carriers and reinsurers, life and pension companies, asset managers and platforms, exchanges and post-trade infrastructure, and the regulated end of digital markets and payments.

What we bring.

  • M&A, capital, and restructuring advice to financial institutions and sponsors
  • Independent advice to boards on regulated transactions and combinations
  • Equity and credit investment via merchant banking, where regulation permits
  • Specialist asset management strategies in financial services equity and credit

Digital markets.

We hold the view that the most consequential financial-services investments of the next decade will be made at the regulated edge of digital markets — in custody, tokenisation, market infrastructure, and the regulated bridges between real-world assets and on-chain capital. Our work in this area is deliberately institutional, deliberately regulated, and deliberately governed.

Speak with our financials team.

For advisory, principal investing, and capital-markets conversations across the financial system, our senior coverage partners welcome introductions.
