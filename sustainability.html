Stewardship of capital, and of the systems it touches.

Sustainability, in our view, is a question of long-term capital stewardship. We integrate environmental, social, and governance considerations into our investment process because they are material to risk-adjusted returns — not because they are fashionable.

"The long view changes what counts as relevant."

How sustainability is integrated.

We do not run a parallel sustainability process. ESG factors — physical and transition risks in energy, labour practices in mining and industrials, governance in regulated financials, data and conduct risks in technology — are considered as part of every diligence we do. Where they are immaterial, we say so. Where they are material, we underwrite to them explicitly.

Where we engage.

Across our merchant banking and asset management practices, we engage actively with management teams and boards on capital allocation, environmental impact, workforce and supply chain practices, governance, and disclosure. Engagement is led by sector specialists, not by an external ESG team operating apart from the investment process.

What we expect of our portfolio.

  • Clear and credible plans for managing material climate-related risks and transition exposures
  • Disciplined capital allocation and a focus on durable returns on capital
  • A culture of physical, mental, and operational safety in industrial and real-asset businesses
  • Rigorous governance over data, conduct, and stakeholder commitments
  • Transparent disclosure to stakeholders — without performative or selective reporting

Sustainability and the real economy.

Many of the most important investments of the next twenty-five years will be in the physical economy — in the supply chains, infrastructure, and operating businesses that quite literally build the future. We believe these investments are, properly underwritten, both attractive and sustainable. They cannot be made by capital that refuses to engage with their complexity.

Philanthropy and stewardship.

Where clients seek to align capital with charitable or stewardship objectives, our wealth practice helps families and foundations design the structures, investment policies, and governance to do so without sacrificing rigour.

Engage with the practice.

For institutional clients and family principals seeking to integrate sustainability considerations into their portfolios, we welcome a confidential conversation.
