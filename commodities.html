The physical supply chain of growth.

Industrial metals, battery materials, and the upstream and midstream businesses that physically produce the next decade of growth. We cover the sector as advisor, principal, and active investor.

"Capital that understands geology, chemistry, and supply chain is rare. We are deliberately built for that."

Sector framework.

Mining and materials sit at the centre of the next investment cycle. The build-out of the electric system, the reshoring of industrial supply chains, and the strategic concerns of governments and corporates have created sustained demand for industrial metals — and a structural under-investment in supply that will take a decade or more to correct.

Where we focus.

  • Copper, nickel, cobalt, lithium, and the battery-materials supply chain
  • Aluminium, zinc, and core industrial metals
  • Rare earths and specialty materials of strategic importance
  • Bulks (iron ore, metallurgical coal) where capital discipline creates returns
  • Downstream processing, refining, and supply-chain infrastructure

How we engage.

We advise mining corporates, sponsors, and sovereign-related institutions through Investment Banking; we invest direct equity and credit through Merchant Banking; and we offer concentrated, specialist exposure to industrial metals via Asset Management.

Speak with our materials team.

For corporate, sponsor, and family-principal conversations across mining and industrial materials, our senior coverage partners welcome introductions.
