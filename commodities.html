Commodities · Physical & financial

Where macro meets physical reality.

Our commodities desk operates across crude oil, refined products, natural gas, and precious and industrial metals — combining physical positions, term contracts, and derivative exposures. The book is run by senior traders with multi-cycle experience, integrated with the firm's broader macro and asset-class views.

I. Crude & Refined Products

Crude, products, structure.

We participate in global crude and refined product markets across Atlantic and Asian basins. Positions span physical cargo flows, term supply contracts, and derivative exposures against fundamental supply-demand views.

Why physical capability matters.

Many commodity participants are screen-only. Our edge in physical commodities comes from operational capability — storage, blending, logistics — that lets us participate in markets the screen cannot reach.
