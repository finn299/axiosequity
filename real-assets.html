Capital, where the physical economy compounds.

Direct and partnership investment in long-life, contracted, and inflation-linked real assets — including energy infrastructure, power, transition assets, transport, logistics, and digital infrastructure.

"Long-life cash flows reward long-duration capital."

The investment thesis.

Real assets sit at the centre of the next investment cycle. Demand is being driven by the build-out of the electric system, the reshoring of industrial supply chains, the growth of digital infrastructure, and the strategic concerns of governments and corporates. Capital is required at scale and on long time horizons. We are deliberately built for both.

Where we invest.

  • Energy infrastructure — midstream, processing, storage, terminals
  • Power generation, transmission, and storage
  • Data centres, fibre, and digital infrastructure platforms
  • Transport, logistics, and supply-chain real estate
  • Utilities and regulated infrastructure assets

How we underwrite.

We underwrite to operating cash flow, regulatory framework, and counterparty quality — not to multiple expansion. Leverage is conservative and explicit. Hold periods are multi-decade by default. The firm invests its own balance sheet alongside every transaction.

Speak with our principals.

For owners, developers, and partners considering long-duration capital for real-asset platforms, our principals welcome a confidential conversation.
