Careers · Join the platform

Build a career across every market.

AXIOS EQUITY is a place for operators and analysts who want to think across asset classes — not within one. We hire people with deep specialization who are interested in how their market connects to the others.

What we look for

Specialist depth.
Integrated mindset.

Every successful hire at AXIOS EQUITY combines deep specialization in one asset class with genuine curiosity about how other markets work. The work requires both — sector expertise alone is insufficient; cross-market generalism alone is dangerous.

→

Multi-cycle experience

Senior hires have operated through at least one full market cycle. Junior hires have an analytical foundation that scales with tenure.

→

Operator orientation

We hire from operators, not pure capital allocators. Bottom-up underwriting capability is essential.

→

Integrative curiosity

A genuine interest in how your asset class interacts with adjacent markets — and willingness to share work across teams.
