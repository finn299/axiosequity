A partnership for practitioners.

We hire investors, advisors, and operators who want the autonomy of a small firm, the discipline of a large one, and a multi-decade view of their own career.

"We hire for judgement. We train for craft."

What we look for.

We hire across our four practices for individuals who combine intellectual rigour with an unusual degree of personal accountability. We do not believe in seat-counting or pyramid models. Every member of the firm is expected to act as a partner — and is given the autonomy and the support to do so.

How we develop people.

Career development at AXIOS EQUITY is hand-built. Senior partners are deliberately available to junior colleagues. Mandates are matched to ambition and ability. Travel, exposure, and decision-making responsibility come earlier than in larger firms — and are accompanied by an explicit framework for risk-taking and accountability.

What we offer.

  • A small, partnership-led environment with deliberate flat structure
  • Direct exposure to clients and to investment and advisory decisions
  • A meaningful, long-term equity participation in the firm over time
  • Comprehensive health, retirement, and family benefits
  • Continuing education support and external certifications
  • A research, technology, and operations platform built for craft
