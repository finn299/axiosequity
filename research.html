Research · Investment process

Process that survives the cycle.

Research at AXIOS EQUITY is not a separate function — it is the operating discipline of the firm. Every position is underwritten bottoms-up, stress-tested against macro, and sized against integrated risk before capital deploys.

The Process

Five stages, applied uniformly.

Whether the position is a crude cargo, a logistics asset, or a digital asset core holding, the same five-stage research process applies. Specialist teams execute; the framework is shared.

01 · ORIGINATE

Source

Operating partners, sector networks, public market screens, and direct outreach generate opportunity flow. Most ideas are eliminated quickly.

02 · UNDERWRITE

Bottom-up

Unit economics, counterparty quality, structural protections, and downside cases drive the initial view. We model assets the way an operator does.

03 · INTEGRATE

Macro overlay

The bottom-up view is stress-tested against the firm's macro framework — growth, inflation, rates — and against the existing portfolio's correlations.

04 · SIZE

Risk-adjusted

Position size is set against integrated risk limits — not against the asset class's standalone risk budget. Concentration is earned, not assumed.

05 · MONITOR

Continuous review

Positions are reviewed monthly; convictions refreshed quarterly. Exit triggers — price, fundamental, or correlation — are defined before entry.

Sector Coverage

Specialists at the asset level.

Each asset class is covered by a specialist team with operating background — not generalist analysts following multiple sectors. Conviction is built from sector tenure, not from research reports.

Energy & Commodities

Senior traders with multi-cycle physical and derivative experience across crude, refined products, and natural gas. Direct relationships with NOC and IOC counterparties.

Real Estate

Former developers and operators across logistics, multi-family, and select commercial. Underwriting from owner perspective, not lender perspective.

Mining & Minerals

Mine geologists, metallurgists, and royalty structuring specialists. Direct relationships with operating teams across producers and developers.

Public Equities

Sector-anchored analysts covering industrials, energy, financials, and select tech. Event-driven specialists focused on capital-structure inflections.

Fixed Income

Sovereign macro, IG credit, and private credit specialists. Focused on duration management and structural credit underwriting.

Digital Assets

Crypto-native specialists with protocol-level knowledge and institutional custody expertise. Treasury operations and on-chain credit underwriting.

What We Won't Do

Discipline of refusal.

As important as what we will underwrite is what we won't. Several categories are excluded by policy — independent of return profile.

×

Naked short volatility

No structures whose payoff is short tail risk without offsetting protection. Strategies that consistently sell volatility without bound have ended firms.

×

High-leverage carry trades

No FX or basis trades that require multi-turn leverage to produce return. Leverage discipline is more important than yield search.

×

Reputational exposure

No counterparties or asset classes whose use creates reputational risk to the firm's long-term standing — even where the trade economics are favorable.
