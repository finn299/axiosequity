Research · Investment Process

Process that
survives the cycle.

Research at AXIOS EQUITY is not a separate function — it is the operating discipline of the firm. Every position is underwritten bottoms-up, stress-tested against macro, and sized against integrated risk before capital deploys.

The Process

Five stages, applied uniformly.

Whether the position is a crude cargo, a logistics asset, or a digital asset core holding, the same five-stage research process applies. Specialist teams execute; the framework is shared.

01 · ORIGINATE

Source

Operating partners, sector networks, public market screens, and direct outreach generate opportunity flow. Most ideas are eliminated quickly.

02 · UNDERWRITE

Bottom-up

Unit economics, counterparty quality, structural protections, and downside cases drive the initial view. We model assets the way an operator does.

03 · INTEGRATE

Macro overlay

The bottom-up view is stress-tested against the firm's macro framework — growth, inflation, rates — and against the existing portfolio's correlations.

04 · SIZE

Risk-adjusted

Position size is set against integrated risk limits — not against the asset class's standalone risk budget. Concentration is earned, not assumed.

05 · MONITOR

Continuous review

Positions are reviewed monthly; convictions refreshed quarterly. Exit triggers — price, fundamental, or correlation — are defined before entry.
