A firm built deliberately.

AXIOS EQUITY is a young partnership organised around an old idea: that an investment firm should be measured by the depth of its relationships and the discipline of its capital, rather than by the speed of its growth.

A Brief Chronology

Milestones of the partnership.

2026

Foundation

AXIOS EQUITY LLC is established as a privately held investment partnership in New Mexico, with founding capital and a partnership charter that anchors the firm's long-duration governance model.

2026

Practice formation

The four practices — Wealth Management, Asset Management, Investment Banking, and Merchant Banking — are formally constituted under a single executive committee.

2026

Risk and governance framework

The firm adopts an independent risk function and a written investment policy applicable to every strategy. Co-investment by the firm's own capital is established as a structural requirement.

2026

Sector coverage

Specialist sector teams are formed across energy and power, mining and materials, real assets and infrastructure, financial services, technology and industrials, and regulated digital markets.

Looking Forward

The decades ahead

The partnership is organised explicitly for the long term. Future milestones will reflect the families, institutions, and operating partners we are privileged to serve.

The work continues.
