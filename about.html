Home/About AXIOS EQUITY

A partnership of thirty years, with a new home.

AXIOS EQUITY is the consolidated investment platform of a senior partner team whose principals have worked together — in private banks, asset managers, advisory houses, and operating businesses — for roughly three decades. In 2026 the partnership relocated its platform to New Mexico, formalising the firm as AXIOS EQUITY LLC and combining its activities under one privately held partnership.

"Independence, alignment, and a horizon measured in decades."

Our founding idea.

The decision to consolidate the partners' work under AXIOS EQUITY came from a simple observation. Most of the institutions families and operating partners would historically have turned to — private banks, advisory firms, principal investors — have drifted toward scale, conflict, and product. The qualities that originally distinguished them — discretion, alignment, independent judgement — have become exceptions rather than expectations.

Our ambition is to be the exception. To run a single, integrated partnership where wealth is stewarded with care, advice is given without conflict, and capital is invested with the patience that compounding requires.

A team that has worked together for decades.

The senior partners and operating principals of AXIOS EQUITY have worked together across multiple firms and cycles for roughly thirty years. Cumulative principal experience across the team exceeds two hundred years. Bringing these relationships under a single, privately held partnership is the work of 2026 and beyond; the relationships themselves are much older.

How we are organised.

AXIOS EQUITY LLC is a privately held partnership organised in New Mexico. The firm is governed by an executive committee of senior partners, supported by a managing partner and a chief investment officer. Risk, compliance, and finance functions are independent of the investing partnerships.

The firm's own capital is invested alongside our clients in every strategy. We are deliberately not a public company; we are deliberately not chasing assets under management for its own sake; we are deliberately structured to optimise for a multi-decade horizon.

The four practices.

Across Wealth Management, Asset Management, Investment Banking (advisory only), and Merchant Banking, the partnership operates with shared values and distinct mandates. Each practice has the autonomy to act on conviction; together, they offer clients a coherent and consistent platform.

What we will not do.

We will not chase mandates that conflict with existing client relationships. We will not market product as advice. We will not underwrite securities or take principal trading positions that create unmanaged conflicts. We will not pursue scale at the expense of judgement. We are not a bank; we do not accept deposits or extend balance-sheet credit.

Registrations.

AXIOS EQUITY pursues firm-level registration with the New Mexico Securities Division as a state-registered investment adviser. Individuals at the firm who provide investment advice are registered as Investment Adviser Representatives, having passed the applicable qualifying examinations (Series 65 or equivalent). M&A advisory is delivered consistent with the M&A broker exemption codified at Section 15(b)(13) of the Securities Exchange Act, and is limited to privately held operating-company transactions of the type the exemption contemplates. Capital-raising activities that involve the placement of securities are conducted exclusively in partnership with separately registered broker-dealers, who perform the regulated activity under their own licences and receive any transaction-based compensation. Please review our disclosures.
