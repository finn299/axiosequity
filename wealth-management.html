A private conversation about your wealth.

Discretionary portfolio management, family office services, and the long-view planning that one's most significant financial decisions deserve. We work for families, founders, and the foundations they create.

"We are most useful when the time horizon is measured in generations, not quarters."

An advisor before a manager.

Our wealth practice begins with a conversation — not a product. Before any capital is invested, we work with you, your family, and your other advisors to understand the purpose of the wealth, the obligations it carries, and the time horizons it must serve. Investment strategy follows from that understanding, not the other way around.

Every relationship is anchored by a senior partner. Every portfolio is built on global, multi-asset research. Every recommendation is open-architecture — chosen for the client, not for us.

Private Wealth Advisory

Discretionary and advisory portfolio management for individuals, families, principals, and the institutions they steward. Mandates are bespoke; risk parameters are explicit; the underlying investments span equities, fixed income, real assets, private markets, and selectively, regulated digital exposures.

  • Bespoke strategic and tactical asset allocation
  • Direct equity and fixed income portfolio construction
  • Selective access to private markets and co-investments
  • Multi-currency and cross-border portfolio management
  • Consolidated reporting across custodians and entities

Family Office Services

For families that have outgrown standalone portfolio advice, we offer an integrated family office function — either as the primary office, or alongside an existing one. Our objective is not to replace what already works; it is to provide institutional infrastructure where it is needed and to leave bespoke decisions where they belong.

  • Governance design, family constitutions, and council facilitation
  • Consolidated wealth reporting and risk monitoring
  • Tax, trust, and structure coordination with external counsel
  • Next-generation education and stewardship programmes
  • Liquidity, lending, and treasury for operating wealth

Wealth & Estate Planning

In partnership with external legal and tax counsel, we coordinate the long-term planning that determines how wealth is held, transferred, and stewarded across generations. Our role is to convene the right specialists, hold the long-term view, and translate decisions into investment strategy.

Philanthropic Advisory

From a first commitment to a multi-decade foundation, we help families and individuals design the structures and the investment policies that will outlast them. Our work covers strategy, governance, mission-aligned investing, and the operational discipline that meaningful philanthropy requires.

How we work

Four principles that shape every mandate.

Principle One
·
A senior partner leads every relationship. There is no team behind a relationship manager — only the partnership itself.
Principle Two
·
Open architecture by design. We are not paid to recommend our own product — we are paid to choose the right one.
Principle Three
·
Risk is the language we speak first. Returns are the consequence of disciplined risk-taking — not the starting point.
Principle Four
·
Time horizons measured in generations. We underwrite to the long term, and we resist the tyranny of the quarter.

A confidential introduction.

Conversations with our wealth partners are held in confidence. Whether you are evaluating a new advisor or formalising a family office, we welcome a discreet first meeting.
