The partnership.

A senior partnership of investors, advisors, and operators — colleagues for roughly three decades — chosen for judgement, experience, and the ability to act with discretion on behalf of those we serve.

The senior partners.

The Executive Committee is responsible for the firm's strategy, governance, and culture. Day-to-day investing and advisory decisions are made by the heads of each practice and their teams. Individual partner biographies are released selectively, by appointment.
