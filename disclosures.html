Any such offer or solicitation, if and when made, will be made only by means of formal offering documents to qualified investors in compliance with applicable law.

Not a bank

AXIOS EQUITY is not a bank, trust company, or insured depository institution. The firm does not accept deposits, does not extend balance-sheet credit, and is not licensed or chartered to engage in banking activities. References on the Site to "investment banking" and "merchant banking" describe historical industry service categories — including advisory, principal investing, and capital structure work — and are not representations that AXIOS EQUITY operates a bank or is licensed as one.

Regulatory status & registrations

AXIOS EQUITY is in the process of completing applicable U.S. registrations. Investment advisory and asset management services described on this Site are or will be offered only in jurisdictions where the firm and its representatives are duly registered, notice-filed, or operating under a valid exemption.

Investment adviser registration

AXIOS EQUITY LLC, as the operating entity, pursues firm-level registration with the New Mexico Securities Division as a state-registered investment adviser. Each individual at the firm who provides investment advice for compensation is registered as an Investment Adviser Representative ("IAR") under New Mexico law (and notice-filed in other states as required), having passed the applicable qualifying examinations (Series 65, or Series 66 in combination with Series 7). Personnel who do not provide investment advice — including operations, technology, finance, and administrative staff — are not required to be IAR-registered and are not held out as such.

M&A advisory

Where AXIOS EQUITY provides mergers and acquisitions advisory services to privately held operating companies, the firm intends to operate consistent with the M&A broker exemption codified at Section 15(b)(13) of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934, as amended, and applicable SEC no-action guidance. Engagements under this exemption are limited to transactions in which a buyer acquires control (typically 25% or more of voting securities, or operational control) of a privately held operating company that is not a shell company. AXIOS EQUITY does not take possession of client funds or securities in these transactions, does not engage in public solicitation, and does not facilitate offerings of unregistered securities in violation of applicable law. Where a transaction falls outside the exemption — for example, an underwritten public offering, a sale of an investment fund interest, or a transaction involving a shell company — the engagement is conducted exclusively through, or in partnership with, separately registered broker-dealers who hold the required licences.

Debt advisory, equity advisory & capital raising

AXIOS EQUITY's compensation for debt advisory, equity advisory, and structuring mandates is structured as fixed retainer, project, and hourly fees, not as transaction-based or success-contingent commissions tied to the placement of securities. Where a mandate requires the actual placement of securities — including private placement to qualified investors, syndication of debt to institutional lenders, or marketing of equity to capital providers — AXIOS EQUITY conducts such activity exclusively in partnership with separately registered broker-dealers (FINRA member firms) or qualified financial counterparties holding the relevant licences. Any transaction-based compensation in such mandates is paid to, and disclosed by, the licensed partner. AXIOS EQUITY does not itself accept transaction-based compensation in connection with the placement of securities other than where it is duly licensed to do so.

Network of licensed partners

The firm maintains long-standing working relationships with a network of separately licensed broker-dealers, registered investment advisers, qualified custodians, transfer agents, trust companies, money-transmission licensees, export-control counsel, and other regulated counterparties. Where an engagement, mandate, or transaction calls for activities the firm itself is not registered or licensed to undertake, AXIOS EQUITY engages or co-engages the appropriate licensed counterparty, and the regulated activity is conducted by that counterparty under its own authorisations. AXIOS EQUITY's role in such engagements is that of advisor, coordinator, and (where applicable) structurer; the regulated activity itself is performed by the licensed partner.

State registrations. AXIOS EQUITY pursues, where applicable, firm and IAR registration with the New Mexico Securities Division and notice filings in other U.S. states in which the firm conducts business. Status updates are available on request.
