Institutional capital, patient research.

Multi-asset and specialist mandates for institutions, family principals, foundations, and endowments — managed by research-led teams with the firm's own capital invested alongside our clients.

"Research-led, conviction-weighted, time-tested."

The investment philosophy.

Markets reward patience, punish leverage, and never forgive the failure to think clearly about risk. Our strategies are built on that conviction. We are deliberately concentrated where we have an edge, deliberately diversified where we do not, and deliberately long-duration in our outlook.

Multi-Asset Strategies

Flagship strategies that combine global equities, fixed income, real assets, and selectively, private market exposures into a single, professionally managed mandate. Designed for institutional and family principals seeking a single-relationship solution governed by an explicit, transparent investment policy.

  • Strategic asset allocation reviewed quarterly
  • Tactical positioning driven by macro and valuation discipline
  • Active management of currency, duration, and credit exposure
  • Defined risk budgets and explicit drawdown tolerances

Global Equities

High-conviction, research-led equity portfolios concentrated in companies with durable competitive positions, strong cash returns on capital, and management teams that allocate as if they own the business — because, often, they do.

  • Global Quality Equity
  • Real Economy Equity (energy, materials, infrastructure)
  • Specialist Sector Equity (financials, industrials)
  • Sustainable and Transition-Aligned Equity

Fixed Income & Credit

From sovereign and investment-grade credit to opportunistic and private credit, our fixed income strategies focus on the unglamorous arithmetic of duration, credit risk, and cash flow. We believe that disciplined credit work — done one issuer at a time — is one of the most reliable sources of long-term institutional return.

  • Global Investment Grade
  • Crossover and Opportunistic Credit
  • Short-Duration and Liquidity Strategies
  • Private Credit Co-Investment (in partnership with Merchant Banking)

Alternative Investments

Selective exposure to alternatives — including real assets, private markets, hedged equity, and regulated digital strategies — chosen for the specific risk and return characteristics they bring to a portfolio, and the operational rigour with which they can be governed.

Risk & governance.

Every strategy is subject to an independent risk function, a documented investment process, and a written investment policy that defines explicitly what we will and will not do with client capital. Discipline is the precondition for performance.
