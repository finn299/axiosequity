Operating Principles

Three commitments.

01

Cash flow before narrative

Every position — real asset or public market — is sized against bottoms-up unit economics, counterparty quality, and structural protections. Thematic and narrative views are layered on top, not relied on alone.

02

Specialist execution, integrated capital

Each asset class is run by people who have operated it for decades. Their conviction must clear the platform's risk framework and earn its allocation against alternatives — none of the teams are ring-fenced.

03

Hold through cycles

Permanent and long-locked capital lets us take illiquidity premia other firms can't, and avoid forced selling at cyclical bottoms. Liquidity is laddered so we deploy when opportunities open.
