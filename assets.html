Home/Sectors/Energy & Power

Capital for the physical economy.

Across advisory, asset management, and merchant banking, we work on upstream, midstream, generation, transmission, storage, and the long electrification cycle. Sector specialism, generalist judgement.

"The energy system is being rebuilt. Capital that understands its physics will be rewarded."

How we cover energy.

The firm's energy and power coverage spans the value chain — from upstream resource development, through midstream gathering, processing, storage, and transportation, to power generation, transmission, distribution, and the rapidly growing storage and balancing layer of the modern grid. We work for corporates, sponsors, sovereigns, and operating partners.

Hydrocarbons in transition.

We believe that hydrocarbons will remain a material part of the energy system for decades and that the assets that produce, process, and move them will continue to require capital, advice, and good governance. We approach the sector pragmatically: disciplined underwriting, attention to operational and environmental risk, and recognition of the long transition arc the system is now traversing.

The electrification build-out.

We are equally active in the build-out of the electric system that the next twenty-five years will require: utility-scale generation (renewable, dispatchable, and firm), transmission and distribution upgrades, large-scale storage, and the industrial supply chains that physically produce all of the above.

Where we add value.

  • Strategic advisory on M&A, divestitures, joint ventures, and capital structure
  • Debt advisory and restructuring for energy operators and sponsors
  • Sponsor and sovereign coverage on infrastructure and platform transactions
  • Direct investment and co-investment via merchant banking
  • Energy-themed equity and credit strategies via asset management
