Liquidity, on institutional terms.

Liquidity solutions for limited partners and general partners — including LP-led portfolio sales, GP-led continuation vehicles, and selective co-investment opportunities offered to qualified institutional partners.

"The right secondary is rarely the loudest one."

Where we are active.

The secondary market has matured into a core liquidity tool for institutional and family-principal investors. We are active across the spectrum — but with a deliberate preference for transactions where deep underwriting and structural creativity add real value, rather than where pricing alone determines the outcome.

Mandates.

  • LP-led portfolio sales and stapled transactions
  • GP-led continuation vehicles and tender offers
  • Single-asset and concentrated continuation transactions
  • Direct secondaries and structured liquidity solutions
  • Selective primary and co-investment for institutional partners

Co-investment.

Where appropriate, we offer co-investment opportunities to qualified institutional partners alongside our merchant banking commitments. Co-investment is offered on aligned, transparent terms; it is not used to subsidise the firm's primary commitments.

Speak with our secondaries team.

For LPs, GPs, and co-investment partners considering a transaction, our principals are available for confidential conversations.
