Framework

Three regime drivers.

Our macro framework reduces complexity to three drivers — growth, inflation, and liquidity — and the policy response that connects them. Each asset class behaves differently across regime quadrants; positioning follows.

G

Growth regime

Expanding, decelerating, recessionary, recovering. Each phase tilts the portfolio toward different asset classes. Pro-cyclicals dominate in expansion; defensives and duration in recession.

I

Inflation regime

Disinflation, stable, accelerating regimes drive real-rate paths and policy choices. Real asset and commodity exposure rises with accelerating inflation; duration with disinflation.

L

Liquidity regime

G4 central bank balance sheets, money market dynamics, and credit creation define liquidity. Risk asset multiples expand with easing liquidity; compress when tightening.

Regional outlook

View by region.

US

United States

Growth supported by services consumption and AI capex. Disinflation stalling above target. Fed easing constrained — long pause more likely than fast cuts.

EU

Europe

Growth running below trend; manufacturing soft. ECB ahead of Fed in cutting cycle. Energy security and industrial competitiveness remain structural concerns.

CN

China

Property deleveraging continues; consumer recovery uncertain. Policy support intermittent. Manufacturing strong; export pressure rising.

JP

Japan

BoJ exiting accommodation; yen carry unwinding has macro consequences. Capital repatriation a tailwind for domestic credit and equity.

EM

Emerging Markets

EM CBs largely ahead of DM in cutting. FX volatility constrains policy; commodity-linked EMs benefit. Selectivity essential.

FR

Frontier & Resource

Critical minerals jurisdictions (Australia, Canada, select LatAm and Africa) benefit from energy transition demand. Political risk requires careful structuring.
