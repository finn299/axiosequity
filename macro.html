Cycles, structures, and the long arc.

Our macro work attempts to separate the cyclical from the structural — and to focus the firm's capital and advice on the structural shifts that will define the decades ahead.

"Most of what is called macro is a story. The work is in finding the part that is mechanism."

The macro view in summary.

We hold the view that the next decade of investment returns will be earned in the real economy — in the physical capital, the operating businesses, and the regulated platforms that intermediate the long-running shifts in growth, energy, technology, and geopolitics. Our portfolios, our advisory work, and our principal investing all reflect that conviction.

The five structural themes.

  1. The physical economy returns. Energy, materials, infrastructure, and industrials are at the centre of the next cycle.
  2. Capital has a cost again. A return to positive real rates favours disciplined balance sheets and durable returns on capital.
  3. Supply-chain reorganisation. Strategic concerns are reshaping where capital is deployed, who deploys it, and on what terms.
  4. Regulated digital markets. The institutional-grade plumbing of the next financial decade is being built — and it is regulated.
  5. The stewardship of wealth. Multi-generational families and institutions are professionalising the operation of capital at scale.

What this means for portfolios.

We favour high-quality equity in real-economy sectors, disciplined credit, real and infrastructure assets with long-duration cash flows, and selective, governed exposure to regulated digital markets. We are cautious about narrative-driven equity, low-quality credit, and unregulated digital exposures.
