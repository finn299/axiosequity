Capital that operates, not just allocates.

Long-duration private capital invested directly alongside our clients and operating partners. Corporate private equity, real assets, infrastructure, and private credit — held to multi-decade horizons, governed as a partnership.

"We back operators. We invest where we would also choose to work."

A merchant banking philosophy.

Our merchant banking practice exists for a simple reason: some of the most attractive risk-adjusted returns in our markets come from owning operating businesses well, for a long time. We deploy patient equity and credit alongside operators we have known and underwritten — and we invest our own capital in everything we do.

Corporate Private Equity

Control and significant minority investments in operating companies — typically in the real economy: energy and power, materials and mining, real assets, financial services, mission-critical industrials, and selectively, regulated digital infrastructure. Hold periods are long; financial engineering is minimal; governance is hands-on.

  • Control buyouts in mid-market and lower-large-cap businesses
  • Significant minority and growth equity investments
  • Carve-outs from corporates and family principals
  • Public-to-private transactions in select circumstances

Real Assets & Infrastructure

Direct and partnership investment in long-life, contracted, and inflation-linked real assets — including energy infrastructure, power generation, transition assets, transport and logistics infrastructure, and digital infrastructure platforms.

  • Energy infrastructure (midstream, storage, processing)
  • Power generation, transmission, and storage
  • Data centres, fibre, and digital infrastructure
  • Transport, logistics, and supply-chain real estate

Private Credit

Senior secured, unitranche, mezzanine, and structured private credit to operating businesses where capital structure matters and where bespoke documentation creates lasting value. Origination is partnership-led; underwriting is fundamental, not flow-based.

  • Direct lending to mid-market operating businesses
  • Asset-backed and asset-based credit
  • Real-asset-backed and infrastructure credit
  • Opportunistic and rescue credit

Secondaries & Co-Investment

Liquidity solutions for limited partners and general partners — including LP-led portfolio sales, GP-led continuation vehicles, and selective co-investment opportunities offered to qualified institutional partners.

Partnership economics.

We invest the firm's capital alongside our partners and clients in every strategy. Co-investment is the rule, not the exception. The objective is alignment by design — and the long-term compounding that alignment makes possible.

Where we invest

Themes of conviction.

Our portfolio activity concentrates on a small number of structural themes. Specialist coverage allows us to act with conviction; patient capital allows us to act with discipline.

T-01

Electrification & Power

The build-out of generation, storage, transmission, and the physical infrastructure of a more electric economy.

T-02

Critical Materials

The industrial metals and supply chains that physically enable the next decade of growth.

T-03

Digital Infrastructure

Data centres, fibre, and connectivity platforms — the physical layer of the digital economy.

T-04

Industrial Defence

Mission-critical industrial systems and dual-use platforms with strategic national relevance.

T-05

Mid-Market Operating

Established, cash-generative operating businesses in resilient corners of the real economy.

T-06

Regulated Digital Markets

Selective, governed exposure to regulated digital asset infrastructure and tokenised real-world capital.
