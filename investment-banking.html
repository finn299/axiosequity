Independent advice on the decisions that matter most.

We advise corporations, governments, families, and institutions on their most consequential strategic and financial decisions — without proprietary capital agendas, without conflicting product mandates, and without distraction from the matter at hand.

"The most valuable thing an advisor can offer a client is the courage to say no."

The advisor's brief.

Our investment banking practice is deliberately structured to remove conflict from advice. We do not underwrite. We do not lend on balance sheet. We are not seeking to place a financing or sell a product. We are seeking to be the most useful seat at our client's table — and the one most willing to disagree.

Mergers & Acquisitions

Buy-side, sell-side, and merger advisory for corporates, family principals, and institutional sponsors. From cross-border combinations to controlling-stake recapitalisations, our role is to define the strategic question first and the execution path second.

  • Sell-side mandates and structured divestitures
  • Buy-side advice and acquisition financing structuring
  • Public and private merger negotiation and execution
  • Joint ventures, strategic alliances, and controlling-stake transactions
  • Cross-border deals and inbound advisory

Debt Advisory & Restructuring

Independent debt advisory to borrowers and to creditors — covering capital structure design, financing execution, covenant negotiation, refinancing strategy, and complex restructurings. Our incentive is alignment with the client; we do not place the paper.

  • Capital structure design and refinancing strategy
  • Private and public debt placement advisory
  • Restructuring, recapitalisation, and special situations
  • Lender, bondholder, and ad-hoc group advisory
  • Distressed M&A and 363 / Chapter 11 advisory

Equity Advisory

Independent equity advice covering pre-IPO planning, listing strategy, follow-on and rights offerings, block transactions, and capital return programmes. We sit beside the issuer, not on the syndicate desk.

Sovereign & Sponsor Coverage

Dedicated coverage of governments, sovereign-related institutions, and large financial sponsors — including strategic mandates, privatisation advice, infrastructure advisory, and sponsor portfolio reviews. Independence is most valuable when the counterparty is most powerful.

Sectors we cover.

Our bankers and sector specialists concentrate on the industries we know best: energy and power, mining and materials, financial services, technology and industrials, and the long-duration real-asset platforms that increasingly intersect with all of them.

How we work with licensed partners.

AXIOS EQUITY operates the advisory and coordinating role; regulated activities that fall outside the firm's own authorisations are conducted by separately registered counterparties. Specifically: compensation for our advisory work is structured as fixed retainer and project fees, not as transaction-based commissions for the placement of securities. Where a mandate requires the actual placement, syndication, or underwriting of securities, AXIOS EQUITY engages or co-engages a separately registered broker-dealer (FINRA member firm) who performs the regulated activity under its own licences. This network is long-standing, deep, and scales to the requirements of the mandate. Please see our disclosures.
