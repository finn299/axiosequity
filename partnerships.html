Capital. Operators. Partnership.

Our merchant banking practice operates as a partnership with the management teams we back. We bring capital, governance, sector intelligence, and the firm's wider platform — and we expect operators to bring the rest.

"The right partnership is more valuable than the right valuation."

How we partner.

Operating partnerships at AXIOS EQUITY are deliberately long-duration. We underwrite to multi-decade ownership; we resist the artificial deadlines of a fund vintage; and we expect to be measured by the businesses we help to build rather than by the marks we report.

What we bring.

  • Patient, aligned capital — invested alongside the firm's own balance sheet
  • Deep sector specialism through our research and advisory teams
  • Governance discipline, including independent risk and audit oversight
  • Access to the firm's wealth, asset management, and advisory practices
  • A network of operators, advisors, and corporates across our coverage sectors

What we ask of partners.

  • Operational excellence as a non-negotiable
  • Disciplined capital allocation and a focus on durable returns on capital
  • Transparent governance and direct relationships with the board
  • Long-term thinking on stakeholders, workforce, and physical and operational safety

The investment process.

Investment opportunities are originated by the merchant banking team and by the firm's wider sector partnerships. Diligence is led by sector specialists, supported by independent functions, and overseen by the investment committee. Final approval rests with the committee; consent rights and governance arrangements are explicit and written before capital is committed.

Introduce yourself.

If you are an operator, a management team, or a seller of an institutional-quality business considering long-duration capital, we welcome a confidential first conversation.
