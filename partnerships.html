Partnerships · Co-investment · Operating

Capital that operates.

AXIOS EQUITY partners with experienced operators across every market we operate in — energy traders, mine operators, real estate developers, infrastructure managers. Capital sits alongside operators with aligned economics, not above them as a passive allocator.

I. Partnership models

Structures we use.

We are flexible on structure but disciplined on alignment. Every partnership ensures operator interests are tied to capital outcomes — through carry, equity, or royalty.

Operating JV

Joint venture with a regional or sector operator who handles execution, while AXIOS EQUITY provides equity capital and platform-level discipline. Operator participates through carry or co-investment.

Co-investment

Direct co-investment alongside an established sponsor or operator on a specific transaction. AXIOS EQUITY underwrites independently; operator runs the asset.

Royalty & streaming

Royalty or streaming positions in real assets — particularly mining — where we participate in production economics without operating responsibility. Useful for downside-protected exposure.

Preferred equity

Preferred equity capital with structural protections, used in real estate and select operating businesses where downside protection and yield-plus-equity are preferred over common.

Structured credit

Private credit secured by real assets or operating cash flows, with covenants and recovery rights. Used selectively where bank capital has retreated.

Platform investment

Equity in operating platforms (e.g., a regional mining royalty company, a logistics operator). Long-duration capital backing experienced management teams.

II. What we look for in partners

Alignment over brand.

Partner selection is the most important decision in real-asset investing. We prioritize operators with multi-cycle experience, structural skin in the game, and an aligned long-duration time horizon.

→

Multi-cycle operating history

Track record through at least one full sector cycle. Capital allocators with no operating background are excluded by policy.

→

Skin in the game

Meaningful operator co-investment alongside our capital. Pure management fee structures without economic alignment are not pursued.

→

Long-duration orientation

Partners willing to hold through cycles. Quick-flip oriented sponsors are mismatched with our capital profile and rarely produce sustained returns.

→

Reputational discipline

Counterparty and partner reputational quality matters. We avoid partnerships whose use creates reputational risk to the firm.
